What It Is

Unhidra Sentinel is a new class of embedded sensors built for quiet awareness. Designed to detect subtle environmental drift -- temperature, light, pressure -- and respond only when those signals break from expected patterns. No chatter. No cloud. Just local intelligence that acts only when action is justified.

How It Works

  • Monitors ambient light, heat, motion, and drift passively
  • Operates offline -- LTE activates only on verified emergency
  • Runs edge logic firmware, fully customizable and brandable

  • To represent your brand, not ours
  • To protect when everything else is silent
